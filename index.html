"The rules aren't hidden. They're just scattered across six different systems that were never built to talk to each other."

Why Foreigners in South Africa Won't Work is an independent policy analysis, not a legal opinion or a political argument. It exists because the people asking "can I just get a job here" are usually met with six different answers from six different sources — immigration law, BBBEE codes, employment equity targets, wage regulation, the scarce-skills list, and remuneration thresholds — none of which were written with each other in mind.

A.J. Steyn walks through each framework in plain terms: what it says, how it's applied in practice, and where it quietly closes doors that look open on paper. No legal jargon left unexplained, no chapter longer than it needs to be.
